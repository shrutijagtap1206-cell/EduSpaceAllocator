# 12. Future Scope & Scaling Roadmap

## 1. IoT Sensor Integration
- **Real-Time Occupancy Sensing**: Deploy lightweight PIR / camera-based edge sensors to detect actual seat utilization and automate no-show cancellations.
- **Smart Door Access Control**: Integrate automated RFID/QR code smart locks synced with verified schedule sessions to enable keyless facility entry.

---

## 2. Dynamic Algorithmic Pricing & Subsidies
- **Micro-Subsidies for CSR Funding**: Enable corporate CSR donors to directly sponsor space rental costs on an hourly basis through automated escrow payments.
- **Off-Peak Dynamic Discounts**: Automatically adjust suggested rental rates based on seasonal demand, time-of-day slots, and local community necessity.

---

## 3. Multi-City & State-Wide Scaling
- **Hierarchical Tenancy Architecture**: Partition spaces, organizations, and administrative boundaries across multiple municipalities (e.g., Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru).
- **Public Transit Route Factoring**: Incorporate GTFS (General Transit Feed Specification) bus and metro route data to compute multi-modal transit friction instead of pure geodesic distance.
