# 01. Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**EduSpace Allocator** is an AI-driven, geospatial Smart City platform developed to address urban educational infrastructure disparities. In rapidly expanding metropolitan areas like Pune, educational NGOs and community learning initiatives face severe spatial shortages, while numerous commercial, municipal, and institutional facilities (community halls, library reading rooms, training centers, weekend academy facilities) remain idle outside peak hours.

EduSpace Allocator bridges this gap by systematically inventorying underutilized physical spaces and mapping them against grassroots learning demands using **geospatial intelligence (PostGIS)**, **unsupervised clustering (K-Means)**, and **bipartite maximum-weight matching**.

---

## 2. Core Stakeholders
1. **Community Learners & NGOs**: Grassroots organizations seeking safe, accessible, budget-compliant classrooms for remedial education, digital literacy, and vocational training.
2. **Space Providers & Property Managers**: Municipal corporations, social trusts, and institutional managers wishing to monetize or donate off-peak facility capacity.
3. **Education Coordinators**: City officials and program directors planning curriculum schedules and tracking attendance.
4. **City Administrators & Donors**: Municipal leaders monitoring public infrastructure efficiency and measuring UN Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) impact.

## 4. Key Highlights
- **Zero-Waste Spatial Economics**: Transforms vacant real estate into vibrant learning hubs without capital expenditure for new construction.
- **Micro-Proximity Matching**: Restricts pupil travel to safe walking or transit distances ($\le 2$ km average).
- **End-to-End Governance**: From initial spatial discovery and constraint validation to automated course scheduling, conflict resolution, and audit logging.
