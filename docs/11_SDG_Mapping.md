# 11. UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment

EduSpace Allocator directly advances three United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):

```
       ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │             UN SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS                │
       ├─────────────────┬─────────────────────┬─────────────────────┤
       │     SDG 4       │       SDG 10        │       SDG 11        │
       │ Quality         │ Reduced             │ Sustainable Cities  │
       │ Education       │ Inequalities        │ & Communities       │
       └─────────────────┴─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┘
```

---

## 1. SDG 4: Quality Education
- **Target 4.1 & 4.5**: Ensure equal access to all levels of education and vocational training for vulnerable populations.
- **EduSpace Contribution**: By locating underutilized spaces within walking distance of low-income communities, the platform provides classroom facilities for 1,035+ students who would otherwise lack safe learning environments.

---

## 2. SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities
- **Target 10.2 & 10.3**: Promote universal social, economic, and political inclusion, eliminating disparities.
- **EduSpace Contribution**: Democratizes access to educational real estate. Small non-profits operate with the spatial infrastructure of established institutions, reducing educational resource inequality across urban wards.

---

## 3. SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities
- **Target 11.3 & 11.7**: Enhance inclusive and sustainable urbanization, providing universal access to safe, inclusive public spaces.
- **EduSpace Contribution**: Eliminates carbon-intensive new construction by optimizing existing civic and commercial square footage. Reduces student commuting travel, mitigating vehicular emissions and traffic congestion.
